# Weapon progression

The active catalogue contains exactly one model per available category in each district, with Power, Rapid and Precision variants. Retired models are removed from inventory browsing, shops, crafting and loot. Representative internal IDs remain stable; all other old IDs are compatibility aliases, not additional items. Save migration combines quantities, retains the highest upgrade, preserves the equipped loadout, returns displaced attachments/mods to inventory, and remaps presets and crafting progress. Repeated loading does not duplicate items or parts.

## Data

`data/weapons.ts` retains legacy stat templates for conversion. `data/weaponProgression.ts` consolidates these into one district/category model, then applies three shared variant profiles. The four independent item properties are `weaponModel`, `weaponVariant`, `rarity`, and `weaponDistrict`. Additional category patterns are generated by district from the small `specialtyModels` table; existing pistols, rifles, SMGs, shotguns and blades keep their original identities.

Actual district order is Neon Row, Rust Yards, Underpass Market, Blacknet Quarter, Helix Ward, Glassline District, Redline Blocks and Skyline Core. The regular catalogue introduces Common/Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, then Prototype from Helix Ward, with Relic at Skyline. Prototype and Relic remain regular quality tiers. Boss Iconics use a separate unique designation and retain their district's underlying quality tier. Retired signature weapons convert into the corresponding district sidearm, whose abilities are shared by its three variants. Quality adds armor penetration and critical damage rather than large damage multipliers. Crafting quantities use the existing rarity scaling, applied once.

## Combat

Power retains the legacy model's raw damage and cadence and gains two armor-penetration points. Rapid has 82% damage and an 82% base interval. Precision has 88% damage, a 96% interval and +12 percentage points of critical chance. Category damage, cadence, accuracy, armor penetration and other traits remain distinct.

Both combat modes use `systems/weaponCombat.ts` to aggregate the weapon and installed parts once, cap critical chance at 60%, and cap extra critical damage at 125%. Character damage receives the variant tradeoff too, preventing fast weapons from winning solely through flat character damage. Expected critical damage keeps both existing deterministic combat systems reproducible. Upgrades scale model stats, but do not multiply the variant's flat critical bonus or make slow weapons slower.

Idle attack speed controls actual attack intervals with the existing minimum interval and speed-bonus cap. Tactical speed uses square-root tempo scaling within the existing weapon attack; it never creates real-time or free turns. Rapid attacks restore one extra RAM, capped by the equipped deck. Precision improves the existing aimed-shot multiplier by 0.15. Power's penetration helps against existing armor. Original signature abilities operate on the model ID, so all variants retain them.

Existing weapon-category XP now contributes at most 5% damage and is earned from actual weapon attacks in field combat as well as idle kills. Switching categories retains the great majority of a build's strength. No new currency, mastery tree or parallel progression is introduced.

## Acquisition and UI

Retired recipe IDs redirect to the replacement model recipe without appearing as duplicate crafting entries. Every active model exposes all three variants, with the same district gates, blueprints and materials. Starter-sidearm variants can also be crafted. Shops expand their existing offers with variant choices and carry a local model per category. New purchases and crafts cannot bypass district access.

Regular local weapon rolls use one current model per category, with three variants, instead of multiplying the whole catalogue by every rarity. Idle enemies have a 1.5% local weapon roll (6% for high-threat enemies); main-job settlement has a 25% local weapon roll. Existing five-percent rare gig rewards and their pity protection remain. Previous-zone or below-floor weapons are removed from advanced idle weapon rolls without removing materials or owned equipment. Legacy weapon drops can roll any variant of their model.

The shared weapon summary is used in inventory, equipment inspection, shops and crafting. It shows variant, rarity, district, raw damage, effective interval, crit, estimated build DPS, field output, mastery and modifiers, with equipped-item differences. Category art is reused with district accents and PWR/RPD/PRC badges. The crafting browser retains pagination and filters and adds a weapon-variant filter.

## Verification

`tests/weapon-progression.test.mjs` checks catalogue completeness, unique IDs, acquisition references, district gates, variant performance at multiple upgrade levels, mastery, tactical roles, legacy saves and UI output. Existing campaign, economy, combat and lifecycle tests cover actual enemies and bosses. `npm test` and `npm run build` are the project-wide checks.
